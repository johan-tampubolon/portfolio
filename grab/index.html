The assistant grew project by project: making it sound local, making its recommendations useful, giving it a human-enough way to chat, and teaching it when to hand off to Support. Each project has its own page; open any of them inline without losing your place.

The problem

Rich data. No way to make it useful.

Small business owners on GrabMerchant struggled to improve performance. They were time-poor, overwhelmed, and unsure where to start.

What merchants kept asking

Why are my sales going down?

What should I do to get more orders?

How do I even start optimizing?

The opportunity: an assistant that answers in natural language, surfaces personalized insights, converts them into low-effort actions — and earns trust through calm, clear language.
