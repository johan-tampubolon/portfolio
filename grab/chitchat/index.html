01 — Challenge

Small talk is still part of the product

Ignore it and the assistant feels robotic. Overdo it and it pretends to be a friend. Either way, trust drops.

Acknowledge, add a little warmth, then offer one useful next step — and never pretend to be human.

warm ≠ pretending to be human

02 — Approach

A pattern for every kind of small talk

Proposed reconstruction of the approach, for review.

1

Name the moments

Greeting, thanks, venting, off-topic, “are you a bot?”

2

One pattern

Acknowledge → brief warmth → one relevant next step.

3

Clear boundaries

Be transparent about being AI; no personal opinions; gently steer back.
