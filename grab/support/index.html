01 — Challenge

Helpful is not the same as guessing

A wrong answer about money or access is worse than no answer. The assistant needs to know when to answer and when to hand off.

Answer what’s documented, say clearly what isn’t, and hand off with context so nobody repeats themselves.

when unsure, say so
